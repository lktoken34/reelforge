Source: supplied `The Barn Wasn’t Empty… _ PART 2 STICKOCALYPSE MOVIE , CHAPTER 1
(THE LAST FARM) — EPS 2_720p.mp4`, available in the session workspace. ffprobe reports
720×1280, 30 fps, H.264 video/AAC audio, container duration 87.074830s. The video remains
separate from this source repository. This is visual observation, not identification of the
creator's production tools, a rights assessment or a claim that our diagnostic equals its quality.

## Inspection method and limits

Extracted and visually inspected scene samples at **0, 7, 14, 21, 28, 35, 42, 49, 56, 63,
70, 77, 84 and 86 seconds**, plus three adjacent-frame bursts: **8.0/8.2/8.4,
35.0/35.2/35.4 and 70.0/70.2/70.4 seconds**. This is 21 unique inspected timestamps.
Frames were reduced to 240×426 for labeled overview/burst sheets, preserving the source
composition to within thumbnail rounding. No audio was reviewed. Sparse bursts reveal local
changes but cannot establish every animation cycle, exact camera path or full-episode timing.
Reference thumbnails are inspection intermediates, not distributed engine assets.

## Character and outline language

Heads are comparatively large and simple; faces use tiny eye/mouth marks. Torsos are narrow,
limbs elongated, hands/shoes reduced to readable geometric pieces. Clothing colors and
accessories carry identity: hat/brown coat versus backpack/burgundy detail. Silhouettes and
pose matter more than dense surface detail. Thin dark borders are visible around heads,
hair/clothing and some props; outlines are not uniformly thick black around every shape.
Most surfaces use flat or restrained shaded color regions. Characters must remain legible
against each shot's value range; outlines alone do not solve contrast.

## Lighting and depth

Interiors use dark warm browns/olive tones, restrained localized bright cones, pale pools on
floor/figures and broad edge darkening. The flashlight cone is visibly soft/translucent;
multiple planes remain dark rather than uniformly illuminated. Exteriors are desaturated
khaki/green with haze and softer distant trees. Foreground silhouettes, grasses, tree trunks,
shape overlap, scale differences and contrast reduction establish layered depth. These are
2D/2.5D visual cues; the samples do not establish the underlying rendering technique.

## Composition, typography and motion

The sequence includes wide establishing shots, isolated character views, a two-subject barn
view, side-on action, a prop/action insert, a map and a title card. Architectural diagonals,
beam direction and negative space guide attention. The portrait frame often leaves room for
upper labels or lower captions rather than filling every region with detail.

Captions are bold condensed-looking uppercase white with dark outline/shadow, generally in
the lower part of the image; important words sometimes turn red (e.g. 28s). The exact font is
not identified. Rule/map overlays use their own bounded panels and restrained accent colors.

Motion observations are limited to the inspected bursts: a cut between 8.2 and 8.4s; rightward
running translation plus limb changes at 35s; small body/head changes at 70s. Stable environment
features in those short bursts help the action read. We do not infer a complete rig, easing
library, precise camera interpolation or real-time rendering from these samples.

## Implications for future ReelForge work

Favor consistent code-authored shape libraries, deliberate silhouette/pose design, controlled
line weight, palette hierarchy, foreground occlusion and limited well-directed motion. Soft
lights, typography, reusable rigs, environments and choreography remain later milestones.
Phase 2A tests only the lower-level capacity to draw clean paths, overlap, alpha, transforms,
clips and simple gradients efficiently. It does not recreate this reference or certify its style.
